About

I'm Ian Munene

I'm a product manager and learning experience designer who builds things people can actually use and learn from. For six years I've led learning design and curriculum teams at Andela, Moringa School and Educate, where I now manage the designers behind EXP, a program that helps Ugandan secondary school scholars reach their life goals. I've also shipped credit and operations products for informal retailers at Kyosk and Proto Energy, from stabilizing a core ERP to lifting a backend's uptime from 85% to 98%. Whatever the problem, I start with the person on the other end, write clear requirements, test early, and stay accountable from first idea to post-launch.
